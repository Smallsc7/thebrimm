# Quickstart: your first run

Confirm the business facts, agree the research question, then run the collection.

## Before you start

Have the Machine installed in Claude Code or Codex using the app download and instructions on your access page and open a session in the project folder you want the packet written into. That
folder is where the other Machines will find it.

Have ready, if you have them: your product or sales page URL, two competitors by name, and
anything real from your own buyers: reviews, support tickets, refund or cancellation reasons,
sales-call notes, survey answers. Twenty of those change the packet more than a thousand public
comments, and twenty, with ten opened discussions, is enough to reach `full` without opening a
single public review page. That matters more than it sounds: the big review sites refuse to be read
by software, so for many businesses your own material is the only route to a settled read. Strip
names, emails, phone numbers, addresses and payment details first. `none` is a fine answer and the
run still works.

## Step 1: fill the block

Use your approved business brief to fill this block. Confirm changes before each re-run, and answer only the research-specific gaps. Write `unknown` wherever
you do not know and `none` where the thing does not exist. Never guess to make it look complete: a
confident wrong answer contaminates the whole read, and every `unknown` becomes a research job.

```
=== GROUND TRUTH: THE BUSINESS THIS RESEARCH IS FOR ===

Do not research this business. These are its facts, so you never guess them and spend
all of your effort on the market instead. Do not repeat these facts back to me.

WHAT IS SOLD
  Product or service, described plainly:
  What it physically is or mechanically does:
  What it does NOT do (be honest; this prevents wasted angles):
  Price points and how people pay:
  How it is delivered or fulfilled:

THE MARKET
  Where buyers are, and the language they buy in:
  Stage: pre-launch / launched-early / selling / scaling / mature:
  Where sales come from today, roughly:

WHAT IS ALREADY BELIEVED ABOUT THE BUYER  (will be tested, not trusted)
  Who buys, in one sentence:
  What happens right before they start looking:
  What they have already tried that did not work:

PROOF THAT EXISTS AND CAN BE DEFENDED
  Real numbers, with their source:
  Reviews, testimonials, case studies, and where they live:
  Credentials, guarantees, real terms:

CONSTRAINTS
  Claims that must not be made (regulated category, platform rules, legal lines):
  Things this business would never say:
  Capacity, inventory, geography or seasonality limits:

THE DECISION THIS RESEARCH MUST CHANGE
  What is being decided, and by when:
=== END GROUND TRUTH ===
```

Two lines carry more weight than the rest. **What it does NOT do** stops the Machine building a
desire your product cannot honestly serve. **The decision this research must change** stops the
output being a document nobody acts on.

## Step 2: say the sentence

In a Claude Code or Codex session with the skill installed, paste the block and send:

> Use the installed research-machine skill on [your market]. Ground truth below. Decision: [what you are deciding].


That is it. It collects, then reads, then recommends, in that order, and keeps the three visibly
separate.

## Step 3: read the packet top down

Coverage first. If it says `partial`, believe it: real evidence, below the depth bar, candidates
ranked as hypotheses, no lead desire named as settled. If it says `blocked`, it tells you what was
missing and the cheapest way to get it. A packet that says "I found three posts and no quotes" is
worth more than a confident one built on the same three posts; `6-EXAMPLE-PACKET/` is exactly that
run, shipped on purpose.

Then the desire map, and the one desire it recommends leading with. You confirm that choice; the
Machine ranks, a person decides.

## Step 4: hand it to the six

The packet is already beside the business packet in your project; Claude Code and Codex write it
there. If you run some Machines on claude.ai, add `PACKET.md` to that Project's knowledge once. The Copy and Static Ads Machines read
it through their feed-the-machine skill; name the packet when you ask them for copy. Point the Offer Machine at it when you run
your territories. `5-WHATS-NEXT.md` has the exact prompts.

## When to run it again

On a pivot: new market, new country, new product, new price band. On a plateau: a winning angle
stops scaling. Quarterly as a baseline. Never weekly. A re-run never overwrites the old packet; it
writes a new dated one and says what changed.
