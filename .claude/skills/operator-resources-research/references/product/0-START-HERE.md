# The Research Machine: start here

Build a source-linked market packet: buyer language, desires, objections, alternatives, gaps and the tests worth considering next. Coverage comes first so you can see what the run actually examined.

## 1. Install and check your download

Open the access page in your delivery email. Choose Codex or Claude Code. Research runs collection scripts on your computer. If your other Machines are in a browser, use **Open Research setup** on the access page. That download contains Research and every file it needs; your other Machines stay where they are.

Unzip the app download and give the whole folder to your AI. Follow its START-HERE guide.

Follow the selected AI's steps on the page or in your download's START-HERE guide. Run the installation check when you want one, after an update, or when a required component is missing or broken. Read its results before confirming the files passed. The practice task is optional, including for a Machine you're using for the first time.

Keep your business brief and customizations when updating. Review changes before replacing existing files. If you get stuck, email andrej@theoperatorstack.io with your AI, the filename and the error.

## 2. Build your brief when you're ready

You can start your current job with the facts you have. Use `operator-start` when you want the complete business interview. Bring any existing brief, Intake Officer result or free-tool output into this business project. Confirm the facts, fill the relevant gaps and approve the summary. You don't need to repeat answers you already confirmed for another Machine in this project.

Save the brief and checkpoint in your AI. Keep different businesses and clients in separate projects. The interview stays complete even when you pause and continue later.

## 3. Run your first useful job

Use the installed research-machine skill in the same business project. In Codex, select $research-machine; in Claude Code, use the installed project skill. Use the brief you provide here. If there is none yet, it works from what you paste in the chat, flags the missing facts and offers the interview once; it never searches connected accounts. Then paste:

> Use research-machine. Load my approved business brief and use it to fill the ground-truth block. Ask only for missing research-specific inputs. Check the required local and web tools before collecting. Preserve sources and raw evidence, report coverage first, label observed findings, inferences and assumptions, then validate and save the packet for the other Machines.

Research is included with the Full Stack and is not sold on its own. It runs in Claude Code or Codex with local files, Python and working web tools. Writing ads and choosing budgets happen in the subsequent work. Keep the packet beside the business brief: market evidence does not replace your confirmed business facts. Validation checks packet structure and traceability. Review the underlying evidence before acting on its conclusions.

## Where to find the working files

The installed `research-machine` skill leads this product's work. These files are inside the installed operator-resources-research skill, under references/product/. Open them through that installed skill:

- `2-QUICKSTART-first-run.md`: The business facts, tool checks and first collection run.
- `3-WHAT-IT-READS.md`: Sources, collection options and coverage limits.
- `4-EXAMPLE-PROMPTS.md`: Prompts for different research questions.
- `5-WHATS-NEXT.md`: How each of the six Machines uses the packet.
- `6-EXAMPLE-PACKET`: A worked example of a saved research packet.

The supporting files are working tools. Open the relevant guide before filling a worksheet or adapting an example. `LICENSE.md` and `DISCLAIMER.md` explain the use of the files and product terms.

## Come back and continue

Open the same AI project and select the installed research-machine skill. Say: “Continue this Machine with the approved brief and checkpoint I provide for this business. If either is missing, ask me to attach it. Tell me where we stopped and continue the next useful job.” If your host cannot retain project files, attach the saved brief and checkpoint again.

Return to the access page in your delivery email for your files and saved steps. If you get stuck, reply to that email with the product, the AI you use and the exact error. You can also email andrej@theoperatorstack.io.
