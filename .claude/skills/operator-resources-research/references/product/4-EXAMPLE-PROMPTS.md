## Narrower jobs

**Before writing a page**
> Use the installed research-machine skill on [your market]. Ground truth below. Decision: the promise the page
> leads with, the order to answer objections in, and the proof each one needs.

**Before changing an offer or a price**
> Use the installed research-machine skill on [your market]. Ground truth below. Decision: whether the packaging
> or the price is the thing buyers are actually resisting.

**When a winning angle stalls**
> Use the installed research-machine skill on [your market]. Ground truth below. Decision: whether the desire pool
> behind our winning angle has saturated, and what the next pool is.

**A quarterly re-read**

> Ground truth below, unchanged. Carry forward what has not moved and mark what has.
