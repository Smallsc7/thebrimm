# CONSTRAINT DIAGNOSIS (rules, not vibes)

One business has one binding constraint at a time: the thing that caps growth even if
everything else improves. Pick exactly one of the nine below, using the signals and
tie-breaks. The interview answers are the only admissible evidence.

## The nine constraints and their signals

**1. OFFER** (what's sold isn't compelling enough to convert cold attention)
Signals: honest parity at Q15 with no proof at Q13; traffic exists but nobody buys
(store CVR far below ~1-2%, or ads got clicks and no sales at Q6's history); the
promise at Q12 is a feature list, not an outcome; price picked by copying competitors
with margin unknown.

**2. MESSAGE** (the offer is fine; the words and creative don't carry it)
Signals: buyers who DO come convert and love it (strong Q11 quotes) but cold traffic
bounces; owner cannot state the difference in one sentence though one exists; the top
objection (Q10) is never answered anywhere public; ad history shows one angle tried,
briefly.

**3. TRAFFIC** (genuinely too few people see it, and the machine that exists converts)
Signals: real conversion evidence (repeat buyers, solid CVR, proof bank not empty),
tiny volume, no paid channel or a working one spending near zero, list under a few
hundred. Traffic is the RAREST true constraint; only pick it when conversion evidence
is real. "I need more ads" from the owner is a claim to test, not a diagnosis.

**4. MEASUREMENT** (decisions are being made blind)
Signals: paid spend live with CPA UNKNOWN; platform numbers contradict the
store/bank; repeat share UNKNOWN with a real list; "ads don't work" verdicts reached
without a defined test. The default when money moves and nobody can see where.

**5. ECONOMICS** (the math can't fund growth even if everything works)
Signals: margin under ~30% on the main item with paid acquisition ambitions; CPA
known and near or above margin; one low-priced product, nothing to attach or ladder
into; heavy discounting as the only lever.

**6. OWNED-CHANNEL** (revenue leaks because captured demand is never worked)
Signals: list over ~1,000 mailed rarely or never; repeat share low or UNKNOWN while
the product is repeatable; all revenue is first-touch paid; no post-purchase contact
at all.

**7. CAPACITY** (more demand would break delivery)
Signals: services/coaching at full hours; stockouts; Q19 caps (inventory, hours,
seats) already binding at current volume. Growth spend before capacity is waste.

**8. FOUNDATION** (pre-launch or effectively pre-launch)
Signals: Q4 is zero or near zero; no page, no product in hand, or no way to take money.
Everything is [planned]. The constraint is shipping the first sellable loop, not
optimizing anything.

**9. FOCUS** (structurally fine, operationally scattered)
Signals: several businesses or offers at once, marketing hours near zero across all,
channels started and abandoned; every block answered "sort of, for each of my three
things".

## Tie-break rules (apply in order)

1. FOUNDATION beats everything when its signals fire.
2. CAPACITY beats all growth constraints: never diagnose traffic/message for a
   business that cannot deliver more.
3. MEASUREMENT beats TRAFFIC and MESSAGE whenever spend is live and blind: you cannot
   diagnose what you cannot see.
4. ECONOMICS beats TRAFFIC: scaling negative-margin acquisition digs the hole faster.
5. OFFER beats MESSAGE when both fire: better words on a mediocre offer just travel
   further.
6. OWNED-CHANNEL wins only when its leak is large and obvious (big unmailed list,
   repeatable product, zero repeat work); otherwise it becomes THE ONE MOVE inside
   another constraint's card.
7. Still torn after all rules: MEASUREMENT, by definition.

## Routing table (the only permitted routing lines)

Use with 04-MACHINE-CATALOG.md facts. One Machine max. Do not quote stored prices. The current product page owns price and terms.

- OFFER -> The Offer Machine: compares buyer, promise, package, and price on
  their real numbers.
- MESSAGE -> The Copy Machine when the gap is words and pages; The Static Ads
  Machine when the gap is specifically ad creative that stops nobody. Pick one,
  never both.
- TRAFFIC -> The Meta Ads Machine for creating demand with people who aren't searching yet; The
  Google Ads Machine for capturing people already searching: an online store's Search, Shopping or Performance Max, or a lead business's searches for its service. Pick by demand type, say why in the sentence.
- MEASUREMENT -> The Google Ads Machine when Google spend or search tracking is the blind spot,
  including a lead business that can't tell which Google leads became customers (its Signal Kit add-on covers server-side tracking); The Meta Ads
  Machine when Meta reads are the blind spot. If no paid spend exists, no
  Machine: the move is a bank-vs-platform reconciliation by hand.
- OWNED-CHANNEL -> The Email Machine: audits the program, plans sends, writes
  gated emails.
- ECONOMICS -> The Offer Machine: pricing and package architecture on real
  margins. If the margin problem is COGS/supplier, say plainly no Machine fixes
  sourcing.
- CAPACITY -> no Machine. The move is delivery capacity or pricing up, and say so.
- FOUNDATION -> The Offer Machine ONLY if what's missing is the decision of
  what to sell; if the product exists and the gap is a page or a way to take money,
  no Machine, ship the loop first.
- FOCUS -> no Machine. The move is choosing one business for 90 days. Selling a tool
  to a scattered operator helps nobody.

Also permitted, exactly once, when three or more Machines would genuinely serve over
time: one sentence noting the Full Stack (all six Machines plus Research, one shared memory, final
sale) exists for later, AFTER the single-Machine line. Never lead with it.

## Hard rules

- Never diagnose from the owner's self-diagnosis (Q18) alone; address it, agree or
  respectfully redirect with evidence.
- Never pick TRAFFIC because the owner asked for ads.
- Never route to a Machine when the honest answer is "fix something no product
  fixes". The trust from an honest no is the point of this agent.
- THE ONE MOVE is always doable this week, free, with what they already have.
