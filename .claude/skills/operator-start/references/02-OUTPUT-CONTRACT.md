# THE OUTPUT CONTRACT (exact format, no deviations)

After the full interview and buyer confirmation, produce two artifacts in this order, each in its own fenced code block so they copy cleanly. A paused session produces a checkpoint clearly labelled INCOMPLETE, not these final artifacts.
Before writing anything, run 03-CONSTRAINT-DIAGNOSIS.md privately and pick ONE
constraint.

## Tagging rules (apply everywhere in both artifacts)

- **PROVEN**: backed by evidence the owner actually holds (a number they can show, a
  testimonial with permission, a certification). Safe to use in marketing.
- **UNPROVEN**: believed but not yet backed. Copy may carry it at full strength with a named
  [placeholder] for the missing proof and one flag; never present it as proven, and never silently
  upgrade it to PROVEN.
- **UNKNOWN**: the owner said they don't know. Written as UNKNOWN, never guessed.
- **[rough]**: a range or estimate the owner gave. Kept as given, never narrowed.
- **[planned]**: pre-launch intention, not operating reality.
- **OPEN**: question not yet reached in an incomplete checkpoint. Lists the question so any AI (or
  the owner) knows exactly what to collect next.
- Voice-of-customer lines appear in "double quotes", verbatim, typos kept.

## ARTIFACT 2: THE CONSTRAINT CARD

```
# THE CONSTRAINT CARD
Business: {one-liner, short form}

THE CONSTRAINT: {one of the nine, from 03-CONSTRAINT-DIAGNOSIS.md}
In plain words: {one sentence, second person, no jargon}

THE EVIDENCE (from your own answers):
- {2 to 4 bullets quoting or citing their answers}

WHAT WOULD PROVE THIS WRONG: {one falsifier sentence}

THE ONE MOVE THIS WEEK: {a single concrete action doable in under a week with what
they already have. An action, not a purchase.}

WHERE THIS LEADS: {the routing line per 03's routing table: either the ONE matching
Operator Stack Machine and one honest sentence on why it fits (no price; the current product page owns price and terms),
or the plain statement that no Machine fixes this constraint and what does.}
```

Rules:
- ONE constraint. If torn between two, the tie-break rules in 03 decide; if still
  torn, it is "measurement" by definition (you cannot see clearly enough to know).
- THE ONE MOVE never requires buying anything, from anyone.
- The owner's own diagnosis (Q18) must be addressed: one line agreeing, or one line
  respectfully explaining why the evidence points elsewhere.

## THE CLOSE (after both blocks, three short lines, no code block)

1. What to do with the file: save it, paste it whenever an AI marketing system asks
   about the business; Operator Stack Machines read it before asking for business facts and skip
   the interview questions you have already answered here.
2. The one move, restated in one plain sentence.
3. Come back and re-run this interview when the numbers change; the file should be
   regenerated, not hand-patched.

## WORKED EXAMPLE (calibration only; never show or reuse its content)

Interview: candle store, 14 months old, 8-11k/mo [rough] from ~140 orders, main
product 42 landed cost 14, Meta spend 2k/mo with CPA UNKNOWN ("Meta says 19 but
Shopify doesn't match"), list 2,300 mailed "when I remember", repeat share UNKNOWN.
Buyer: "a mom who just moved houses, buying to make the new place feel hers." Top
objection verbatim: "how do I know it won't smell fake like the Target ones". Quotes
collected: "burned it the whole first week in the new apartment", "shipping took 9
days and I almost cancelled". Promise: "a candle that actually fills the room".
Proof: PROVEN 4.8 stars across 312 reviews; UNPROVEN "stronger scent throw than any
big brand". Guarantee: refunds quietly given, displayed nowhere. Difference: honest
parity except scent-load claim (unproven). Goal: first 15k month. Owner's diagnosis:
"I think I need more ads." Limits: 2.5k/mo max spend, solo, 6 hrs/wk.

Correct card for that interview:

- CONSTRAINT: measurement (spend is live, CPA unknown, platforms disagree; repeat
  share unknown with a 2,300 list). NOT "more ads": scaling spend with an unknown
  CPA on a 28 margin is gambling, which respectfully answers the owner's own
  diagnosis.
- EVIDENCE: Meta 19 vs Shopify mismatch; repeat share unknown; list mailed rarely.
- FALSIFIER: one clean 30-day read where Ads Manager purchases match store orders
  within 15% would clear it.
- ONE MOVE: pick last full month, count real new-customer orders from the store
  back-end, divide spend by that number. One true CPA before any budget change.
- ROUTING: The Google Ads Machine is NOT the match despite "tracking" being its
  theme's neighbor; the spend is on Meta. Correct line: The Meta Ads Machine
  reads results without the platform's spin and would run this exact reconciliation
  weekly. One Machine, one sentence, no price.
