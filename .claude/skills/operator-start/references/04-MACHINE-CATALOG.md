# Operator Stack product scope

Use current product pages for prices and purchase terms. Never call stored prices today's prices.

The six paid Machines are complete systems of skills, references, templates and supporting files. A one-time purchase includes lifetime updates. Use the access page and its complete installation guide. Codex and Claude Code install into a business project. Claude browser uses the complete plugin; ChatGPT uses the individual skill ZIPs, including product resources. Both require the relevant upload option and successful installed-resource checks. Do not promise every AI can run the complete product or name a fixed install/interview duration. Provider accounts, features and usage limits are separate.

- Offer Machine: develops or improves an offer, with economics, a workbook, selling materials and required reviews. The buyer owns the commercial decision.
- Copy Machine: writes and reviews commercial copy using the business context, customer language and source material. Funnel Architect is an optional architecture/asset-brief add-on, not a published website.
- Static Ads Machine: develops concepts, image prompts and reviews. The customer still renders and checks the final images. Conversion Layout Kit is an optional Figma layout library.
- Email Machine: audits an email program, plans flows and campaigns, and writes reviewed emails, for online stores, service and local businesses, coaches and experts, and B2B. Send Files is an optional editable Figma email-template library; implementation happens in the email platform.
- Meta Ads Machine: audits evidence and recommends account or test changes. The buyer approves external changes. Kill/Scale Engine adds economics-backed verdicts when data supports them.
- Google Ads Machine: for online stores (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, feeds) and lead businesses (services, local, B2B, coaching), judged on qualified leads, booked calls and won deals. Signal Kit supports tracking diagnosis and implementation planning.

Full Stack includes all six Machines plus Research Machine. Research is not sold separately and requires local files, Python and relevant web/collection tools in Codex or Claude Code. The other six Machines can be installed in Claude or ChatGPT in the browser; install Research separately in an app. Toolbelt includes all five add-ons, including the two Figma libraries.

Keep one business per project. Bring BUSINESS-MEMORY.md and a checkpoint to the full interview. The Machine confirms existing facts and asks only missing or product-specific questions. Your answers stay in your AI. No system promises revenue or error-free results, and no recommendation authorizes publishing, sending or spending.

If the constraint is outside these scopes, give a useful non-purchase next action. Never invent product capabilities to make a recommendation fit.
