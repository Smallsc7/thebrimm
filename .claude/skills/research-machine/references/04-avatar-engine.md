# Avatar engine

Building the smallest set of avatars that carry genuinely different purchase decisions, and the schema every one of them fills.

## The governing rule

Research the broadest reusable buyer group that shares one purchase-decision spine, then make each execution specific to a buyer in a moment. Do not create a new avatar because the age, job, country, scene or hook can be described differently.

The test, applied to every proposed distinction:

> Would this change the problem or outcome, the failed alternative or mechanism, the awareness, the language, the proof or trust required, the offer or purchase path, the claim boundary, the economics, or the creative needed to make the sale?

- **No**: keep one avatar. Encode the specificity as a trigger, scene, hook or format instead.
- **Yes**: make it a distinct avatar and name exactly which variable changes.
- **Unknown**: mark it a hypothesis. Do not promote it to truth before evidence or a test resolves it.

This is a research-efficiency rule, not an anti-research rule. Persona proliferation is the most common way a research phase produces a lot of pages and no decisions.

## What an avatar is built from

Five categories, in this priority order. The order is the method.

| Rank | Category | Use as the core? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Desires: what they say they want | Yes, the default starting point |
| 2 | Experiences: what they have been through | Yes, a good second |
| 3 | Behaviours: what they observably do | Good for sub-avatars |
| 4 | Emotions: how they feel | Harder to target directly |
| 5 | Demographics: age, gender, location, income | **Last.** It alienates and eliminates |

Demographics are usually implied by the other four and rarely need stating. They belong in an avatar only when they change the decision, the proof, the language, the offer, the eligibility or the execution.

**Core avatar**: pick ONE category as the spine. The form is "people who [core desire or problem]", not "Sarah, 35, marketing manager, drives a Honda". Never combine more than one desire in a core avatar. Do not use a raw desire that is too broad to build under, and do not go so narrow that nothing can be built under it.

**Sub-avatars**: the core avatar plus any of the other four categories. Trigger events are the most common axis but not the only one; behaviours and emotions produce sub-avatars that are often more relatable than triggers, because there is nothing more recognisable than what someone actually does.

**Beliefs are not a category.** When a belief shows up, unpack it: what happened to make them believe it, how does it make them feel, what do they do because of it. That resolves it back into an experience, an emotion or a behaviour.

## The sub-avatar is the angle

Once sub-avatars are built properly, the angles already exist. A well-built sub-avatar suggests its own angle, which is why the two are developed together.

**Do not collapse them when reading results.** A hook that loses has not told you the segment is wrong. Buyer, angle, concept, format, execution, offer and destination are separate variables, and a single losing hook implicates all of them at once. Attributing the loss to the segment requires a controlled comparison, where the buyer is the thing that varied and enough evidence accumulated to separate it from noise. Keep the fields separate in the packet precisely so that attribution stays possible later; a packet that merges them destroys the information a test would need.

Keep the distinction between concept and angle. The concept is internal, the format or structure, and it carries little weight. The angle is external, the buyer's reason to act, and it carries most of it. A format with no angle is not a test.

## The schema

Every avatar in the packet fills the canonical schema bundled with this skill. Do not invent a local variant.

The canonical definition, with the full wording of every field and the copy-paste template, is `references/08-avatar-schema.md`, bundled with this skill. That file owns the schema. What follows is the working summary, not a second source of truth: if the two disagree, the schema file wins.

The schema is **eighteen numbered fields, 1 to 18, plus the why-chain inserted as 14b: nineteen research entries in total.** 14b was inserted rather than renumbering the rest. A separate launch-readiness field exists for organisations that attach release data to an avatar; it is optional, out of scope for research, and must never be invented here.

Surface, fields 1 to 11: persona sentence, trigger event, failed alternatives, awareness stage, proof path, angle, offer frame, hook promise, format, stage, native and cultural context.

Depth, fields 12 to 17 including 14b, and these are the ones that get skipped: limiting belief to enabling belief; identity anchor before and after; pain ladder and desire hierarchy with hell and heaven as scenes; the written-out why-chain; emotional state at the trigger and the cost of inaction; near-purchase objections each mapped to the response it needs; value sensitivity.

Evidence, field 18: evidence status with its source. This is what makes an avatar auditable rather than a guess in a template.

Eleven surface fields plus fields 12 to 18 and the inserted 14b is nineteen research entries. Launch-readiness is an optional house extension: out of scope here and never invented. If your avatar is missing entries, name which ones rather than renumbering.

The completeness bar: the four full-sentence fields (persona, angle, offer, hook promise), the depth fields including the why-chain, and the evidence field. An avatar with no evidence status is not complete, it is a guess in a template.

## The stop rule

Stop avatar research and move to concept development when all of these are true:

1. The shared purchase-decision spine is sourced, or its assumptions are stated explicitly.
2. The packet is complete enough to write credible, source-bounded copy.
3. The evidence supports at least one meaningfully distinct test, without inventing a new avatar for every scene.
4. Another research pass is unlikely to change the chosen problem, mechanism, proof, offer or destination.
5. What is still uncertain is cheaper and safer to resolve with a test, an interview or a support review than with more desk research.

Stopping does not authorise spend. It authorises the next bounded learning action.

## When to go deeper instead of stopping

Escalate past the minimum when any of these is material: a new country, language or culture; buyer and user being different people; a candidate segment with a genuinely different outcome, severity, failed alternative or mechanism question; a claim-sensitive or regulated category; or a purchase path that changes (subscription versus one-off, self-serve versus assisted).

## Ordering personas against angles

If a known buyer is not converting, do not abandon the persona first. Audit the angle and the offer against that persona before widening. Most "the persona is wrong" conclusions are angle failures wearing a persona costume.
